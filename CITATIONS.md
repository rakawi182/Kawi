# Sources and citations

Everything the app quotes or implements, as credited inside `index.html`. Check the primary
editions before citing any excerpt: the transliterations are normalized to this app's IAST and
the Indonesian renderings are free translations written for the app.

## Script and encoding
- Perdana, A. & Nurwansah, A. (2020). *Proposal to encode Kawi*. Unicode L2/20-284R.
  Source of the words, sequences and figure numbers in the "Composites & sequences" tables.
- Unicode Standard, Kawi block U+11F00–U+11F5F (code chart U11F00, Unicode 18.0).
- Noto Sans Kawi 1.000, © 2023 The Noto Project Authors, SIL OFL 1.1.
  https://github.com/notofonts/kawi

## Kakawin excerpts and translations
- Jákl, J. (2020). *Archipel* 100. Transliterations of the Sumanasāntaka excerpts; cites the
  editions of Worsley et al. (2013), Teeuw & Robson (2005) and Robson (2016).
- Hunter, T. (2001). *BKI* 157. Readings of Wṛttasañcaya (Tanakung) and Rāmāyaṇa verses.
  Compare with Kern (1875) and Hooykaas.
- Zoetmulder, P. J. (1957). "Kawi and kekawin". *BKI* 113: 64–68. Manggala (Sumanasāntaka,
  Kālāntaka, Śiwarātrikalpa/Lubdhaka), verses on poets and *laṅö*, and Bhāratayuddha 10.12–13.
- Worsley, P. (2022). *Wacana* 23(2). Fragments quoted with canto.verse references.
